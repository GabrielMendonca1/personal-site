# Writing editorial guide

These pieces are working drafts, not publication-ready claims. They interpret Gabriel's notes in English and deliberately distinguish principles, proposals, and completed evidence.

## Draft map

### My first contact with software — 2021

- **Confirmed by Gabriel:** First contact with software and interest in machine learning in 2021.
- **Evidence still missing:** The first scene, tools or languages, the source of the machine-learning interest, one real attempt, and the question carried into 2022.
- **Editorial boundary:** A retrospective scaffold, not a contemporary 2021 publication. Do not infer a course, project, or technical milestone from later biographical information.

### My first contact with AI — 2022

- **Confirmed by Gabriel:** First contact with AI in 2022.
- **Evidence still missing:** What “contact” means, the specific system, first task, original versus reconstructed evidence, and the next real step.
- **Editorial boundary:** Follow Gabriel's current chronology; older retrospective notes are not sufficient to attribute a tool or a launch date. Clearly separate his original impression from hindsight.

### The model became a subroutine; the loop became the unit of work

- **Thesis:** The dependable unit in agentic work is a bounded inspect–decide–act–verify loop, not an isolated model call.
- **What this draft changes:** Moves the argument from prompt technique to operational design; separates loop design from operation; makes stopping and escalation first-class outcomes.
- **Evidence still missing:** One safe manually executed loop, the actual minimal template, a precise designer/operator boundary, and a qualitative human checkpoint.
- **Source basis:** Gabriel's private notes on loop engineering, design versus operation, and a proposed validation template.

### A second brain for thinking, not just remembering

- **Thesis:** A second brain earns its place by returning connected thoughts and useful questions, while leaving authority with the person.
- **What this draft changes:** Reframes memory as a thinking surface; distinguishes second mind, operator, and external conscience; adds limits on proactivity and memory lifecycles.
- **Evidence still missing:** A safe capture example, challenge and consent boundaries, an interruption policy, and behavior for temporary or superseded context.
- **Source basis:** Gabriel's private notes on thinking surfaces, lightweight capture, proactive questions, and human authority.

### What to build when the labs build the models

- **Thesis:** Smaller teams should make general models reliable inside real workflows rather than imitate the labs building foundation models.
- **What this draft changes:** Grounds the application-layer thesis in an anonymized construction flow; draws a boundary between probabilistic model work and deterministic controls; treats platform breadth, services, portability, and human impact as open tensions.
- **Evidence still missing:** One publishable workflow handoff, accurate maturity labels, evidence of a reusable primitive across workflows, a model-switching comparison, and a current public statement of ARCA's scope.
- **Source basis:** ARCA's founding thesis and private architecture/workflow notes. Only the generalized, hypothetical process belongs in the article; internal project identities and documents do not.

### Context is not accumulation

- **Thesis:** Context engineering chooses what is permanent, retrieved, structured, temporary, or excluded instead of maximizing prompt volume.
- **What this draft changes:** Organizes context by lifecycle and runtime; presents compact push plus deliberate pull as a proposal; emphasizes provenance, absence tests, and secrets exclusion.
- **Evidence still missing:** A safe end-to-end fact journey, the minimum permanent invariants, failure behavior for missing retrieval, and tested handling of stale or contradictory material.
- **Source basis:** Gabriel's private notes on progressive simplification, distinct context lifecycles, selective retrieval, and deterministic records.

### Time is the currency of life

- **Thesis:** Decisions should be priced by the finite life and attention they consume, without turning life into an optimization ledger.
- **What this draft changes:** Connects the principle to calendar placement and review; develops the accounting objection; makes happiness, rest, relationships, body, mind, and faith ends rather than productivity inputs.
- **Evidence still missing:** One private-safe trade-off, Gabriel's minimal review questions, an external challenge to the currency metaphor, and a bounded trial focused on attention and burden rather than output.
- **Source basis:** Gabriel's principle that time is irreplaceable and private reflections on happiness, attention, scheduling, and review. Personal episodes remain private until deliberately rewritten.

## Edit checklist

- Confirm every first-person statement still reflects Gabriel's present view.
- Resolve every `DraftNote`; remove it or replace it with verified, public-safe material.
- Label proposals, architecture targets, and experiments as such. Do not imply deployment, scale, performance, or outcomes without evidence.
- Check that examples contain no client names, private documents, commercial figures, credentials, access details, or third-party personal information.
- Preserve the distinction between model judgment and deterministic calculation, permissions, logs, and approvals.
- Prefer links to public sources if external claims are later introduced; do not expose internal source identifiers or vault paths.
- Review title, description, lede, and ending together for repetition.
- Read each piece aloud for Gabriel's voice and remove unnecessary framework language.

## Publish checklist

- Obtain Gabriel's explicit publication approval for each article.
- Set a truthful publication date and change `status` only when the article is approved.
- Verify all factual claims, quotations, links, names, and product maturity statements.
- Confirm editorial notes are absent from the published rendering.
- Check the table of contents, heading anchors, demo keyboard behavior, reduced motion, mobile layout, and figure caption.
- Preview draft metadata/noindex behavior before publication, then verify the final canonical URL and listing.
- Perform a final privacy and confidentiality pass independent of the prose edit.
